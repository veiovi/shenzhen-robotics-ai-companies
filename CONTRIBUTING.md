# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list accurate. Send additions and corrections as issues or pull requests.

A company qualifies only if its headquarters or registered head office is in Shenzhen. Confirm this with the company's own website, a stock-exchange filing or a reputable news source, and include that link as the source. Write one neutral line of up to 120 characters saying what the company makes. Leave out marketing language, funding figures and claims of being first, biggest or leading. Add a district only when an official address confirms it.

To add or correct an entry, edit data/companies.json (the CSV is generated from it) or open an issue with the company name, official website and source link. Moves, closures and name changes matter as much as new companies.

We check every source before merging and may decline entries we can't verify. A listing doesn't mean a company endorses or has any relationship with Shenzhen Assistant, and nobody can pay to be listed.
