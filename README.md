## How entries are chosen

We list a company only when its own website, a stock-exchange filing or a reputable news source places its headquarters or registered head office in Shenzhen. Descriptions cover what each company makes. We include a district only when an official address confirms it.

A listing does not mean a company endorses or partners with Shenzhen Assistant, or has any other relationship with it. We receive nothing for listing anyone.
